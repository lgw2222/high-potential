The Mystery Unfolds

What is High Potential about? ▶

High Potential is an American crime drama series created by Drew Goddard that premiered on ABC on September 17, 2024. Based on the popular French series "HPI," the show follows Morgan Gillory, a brilliant single mother of three who works as a cleaning lady at the Los Angeles Police Department.

When Morgan unexpectedly solves a complex case using her unorthodox thinking and keen observational skills, she is recruited as a civilian consultant for the LAPD's Major Crimes Division. Her partnership with seasoned detective Adam Karadec creates an unlikely yet unstoppable team that tackles some of the department's most challenging cases.

Why Should You Watch?

Want a brilliant protagonist? ▶

Morgan's genius IQ and unique perspective bring fresh energy to the crime procedural genre. She sees solutions others miss.

Curious about the dynamic partnership? ▶

The chemistry between Morgan and Karadec creates compelling television with humor and heart. Their contrast drives the show forward.

Who created this series? ▶

Drew Goddard, the mind behind "The Good Place" and "The Martian." That's the level of writing talent here.

Are the cases actually engaging? ▶

New cases each episode keep the story moving with clever solutions and twists. You'll be trying to solve them before Morgan does.

Is it just procedural drama? ▶

No. It's the perfect balance of drama, comedy, and procedural elements. It makes you laugh, think, and stay on the edge of your seat.

Will it be around long-term? ▶

Critical acclaim and viewer love position it as a standout series. Already renewed for Season 2 that premiered in September 2025.
